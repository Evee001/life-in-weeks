          How this is calculated
          • Years left = life expectancy minus your age. A year is 365.25 days, so leap years are counted.
          • Work and commute only happen on workdays, so they are averaged across the week (8 hours × 5 days ÷ 7 = 5.7 hours a day). They stop when you retire.
          • Everything else happens every day for the rest of your life.
          • Purposeful time is whatever is left of the 24 hours. It is added up separately for your working years and your retired years.
          • The starting hours are round averages for a working adult, not survey data. Change them to match your own week.
